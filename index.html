Parsa Rangriz

PhD student in Mathematics, UC San Diego

I am a Mathematics PhD student at UC San Diego, advised by Todd Kemp. Previously, I received an MMath in Statistics from the University of Waterloo, advised by Aukosh Jagannath, and a BSc in Physics (minor in Mathematics) from Sharif University of Technology.

My research is in high-dimensional probability and mathematical physics: random matrix theory, stochastic analysis, and spin glasses, with connections to the dynamics of learning in high-dimensional models. I'm also interested in the Gaussian free field, Liouville quantum gravity, and conformal field theory.
